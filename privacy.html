PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 30, 2026 • Compliant with Global Data Standards & Meta Platforms

Our Privacy Commitment to You

At SnapzDealz Inc., your trust is our most valuable asset. We treat your personal data with extreme care. We will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information, phone numbers, or email addresses to marketing brokers or third parties. All sensitive credentials are encrypted using bank-grade salted SHA-256 cryptography.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our store, create an account, or complete a checkout, we collect only the necessary information to provide a seamless shopping experience:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, delivery address, city, contact phone number, and email address.
  • Account Credentials: If you register a VIP account, your password is cryptographically salted and hashed with SHA-256 before storage. Plaintext passwords are never saved.
  • Order History: Products purchased, quantities, billing totals, order status, courier waypoint history, and payment method selection.
  • Device & Analytics: IP address, browser type, and operating system collected anonymously to safeguard against brute-force attacks and fraudulent transactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate e-commerce purposes:

  • Processing, packaging, and dispatching your orders through verified courier partners.
  • Sending automated order receipts, 6-digit verification OTP codes, and shipping notifications via email or WhatsApp.
  • Customer support assistance when you contact our helpline or live chat.
  • Preventing unauthorized logins and protecting against payment fraud.

3. Cryptographic Security & Data Protection

We employ military-grade security architectures to keep your transactions safe:

256-Bit SSL Encryption
All web traffic between your browser and our store travels over encrypted HTTPS protocols.
Salted SHA-256 Hashing
Passwords undergo native cryptographic hashing, making rainbow-table and brute-force cracking mathematically impossible.

4. Payment & Financial Privacy

For your safety, SnapzDealz does not store your credit card, debit card, or banking PINs.

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): No financial details are required online. You inspect and pay your delivery rider at your doorstep.
  • Digital Wallets (Easypaisa / Jazzcash / Cards): Processed securely via authorized, PCI-DSS compliant financial institutions.

5. Automated Communications (WhatsApp & SMS)

When placing an order, you consent to receive critical transactional updates (order confirmation, courier tracking link, and delivery OTP) to your provided phone number via WhatsApp or SMS. You may opt out of promotional messages at any time by replying "STOP".

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use lightweight session cookies and browser storage strictly to remember your Shopping Cart items, Wishlist items, and active login state across page reloads. We do not use intrusive spyware.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You maintain full ownership of your data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Request an export copy of the personal information stored in your profile.
  • Request the permanent purge and deletion of your account and address records.
  • Update or correct inaccurate contact information.

To request account deletion or data inquiry, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@snapzdealz.com.

Have Questions About Your Privacy?

Our dedicated privacy and compliance team is available to assist you 24/7.
